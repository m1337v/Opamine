## Preservation invariants

- Primary roots use `/var/containers/Bundle/Application/.jbroot-` plus 16
  hexadecimal digits.  The low byte is the XOR checksum of bytes 1 through 7.
- Secondary roots use the matching
  `/var/mobile/Containers/Shared/AppGroup/.jbroot-` name.  The primary
  `private/var` and secondary `.jbroot` symlinks preserve their paired-root
  contract.  `/var/jb` is only a compatibility seam.
- Basebin trust is generated by walking `JBROOT_PATH("/basebin/")`,
  randomizing cdhashes, and uploading with `BASEBIN_TRUSTCACHE_UUID`; the
  static `basebin.tc` loader is not active.  The captured ignored artifact is
  version 1, 29 entries, 662 bytes, SHA-256
  `8b6b86c76f96c328bb739a66ecaebb1b5b75a0194eca5f5a45bd846e3c93eb53`.
- The trust-cache uploader retains its `0x4000` allocation cap and UUID-based
  replacement path.  The target HEAD contains the historical same-size write
  that WP1 repairs; the verifier accepts the historical form or the bounded
  `tcSize - ksizeof(trustcache)` form while WP1 is in progress.
- The five policy modes remain distinct and ordered as `stock`, `blacklist`,
  `whitelist`, `hiddenwhitelist`, and `blacklistallowlist`.  Mode and legacy
  list paths stay under `RootHide`, and launchd/systemhook, hidden injection,
  selected-tweak, and caller-sensitive hiding remain one policy system.
